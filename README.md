# FreCre

**A Match-3 Boss Battle RPG Where Every Turn Counts!**

FreCre is a single-player puzzle RPG developed with **Unity and C#**, combining match-3 gameplay with strategic, turn-based boss battles.

Build a team of five unique characters, match elemental blocks to unleash powerful skills, and deal as much damage as possible within **25 turns**!

Experiment with different team compositions, discover powerful synergies, and challenge yourself to achieve a new high score.

## Features

### Strategic Match-3 Combat

- A **6×5 puzzle board** with five elements: Fire, Water, Grass, Light, and Dark.
- Match 3, 4, or 5 blocks to activate different character skills.
- Chain matches together to create powerful combos.
- Make strategic decisions to maximize damage within a limited number of turns.

### Five-Character Party System

- Build a team of five characters with unique abilities and roles.
- Discover synergies between characters and elements.
- Customize your party composition and action order.
- Use active skills to support your strategy.

### Challenging Boss Battles

- Fight powerful bosses with unique mechanics and attack patterns.
- Adapt your strategy to each boss's abilities.
- Survive enemy attacks while dealing as much damage as possible.
- Aim for higher scores and battle ranks within **25 turns**.

### Character & Relic Collection

- Summon characters and relics through the gacha system.
- Level up and awaken characters to strengthen their abilities.
- Equip relics to customize your team.
- Experiment with different builds and strategies.

### Score-Based Progression

- Challenge bosses to achieve higher scores.
- Earn rewards through battle performance.
- Improve your team and attempt new high scores.

## How to Play

1. **Match Elements** — Swap adjacent blocks to create matches of 3 or more.
2. **Activate Skills** — Different match sizes trigger different character abilities.
3. **Use Active Abilities** — Strengthen your team, heal, shield, or manipulate the board.
4. **Fight the Boss** — Adapt to the boss's attack patterns and mechanics.
5. **Maximize Your Score** — Make every turn count!
